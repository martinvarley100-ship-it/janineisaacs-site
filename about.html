About

Coaching for women navigating relationship breakdown and what comes after

Janine Isaacs is a coach working with women who are in the middle of a relationship ending, have recently left, or are still carrying the weight of one that changed them — and haven't yet found their way back to themselves.

Her approach is specific. She is not a therapist. She does not process the past or give advice. She watches for the moments when your body tells one truth and your words tell another — and she helps you see what you are doing, so you can choose differently. Most clients find this works faster than they expect, because it does not ask them to become someone new. It helps them remember who they already are.

Background

Janine spent fifteen years as a medical negligence solicitor, named in Legal 500 and Chambers and Partners, and a partner at one of Manchester's most respected firms. In 2019, at fifty-five, she left a twenty-three year relationship and found herself starting over.

She did not coach her way through it. She lived through it, and found her way out the other side. She trained as a coach because she had been the woman sitting where her clients sit — and she knows the difference between advice that sounds right and support that actually changes something.

She has climbed Kilimanjaro, cycled 350 kilometres across the Jordanian desert, and stood in a frozen waterfall in the Polish mountains. She is also a grandmother. She is not who the label "life coach" usually brings to mind.

Working alongside family lawyers, mediators and financial advisors

A client navigating separation who has this kind of support shows up to legal and financial meetings more focused, more decisive, and better able to act on the advice she is receiving. If you work with women in this situation and would like to talk about how to support shared clients, the door is open.
