This is for you if

You've come through a difficult relationship, one that required you to stay small, dim your light, or question your worth. You don't need someone to fix you. You need someone to show you where you're still shrinking on autopilot, and help you find your way back to her.

You're ready if you recognise yourself in any of this:

  • You know something has to change but every time you get close to the decision, the fear of the unknown pulls you back to the familiar, even when the familiar is making you smaller.
  • Part of you wonders whether the peace of just walking away, even on the wrong terms, might be worth more than the fight.
  • You're facing things you've never faced alone before, finances, a new house, a first holiday with the children, a car to buy, and nobody tells you that these practical things carry grief too.
  • You find yourself mourning the good times, the version of the relationship that existed before everything changed, and you feel ashamed of that, because you're not sure you're allowed to grieve something that also hurt you.
  • The friendships and family ties that have shifted or disappeared in the fallout feel like another loss nobody warned you about. Your social world was built around a couple, and now you don't quite fit the same spaces.
  • Everyone has an opinion. Your friends mean well. Your family means well. But their advice comes shaped by their own story, and when you try to follow it, something in you knows it isn't right for you.

You're not here because you're broken. You're here because part of you remembers who you were before you started making yourself smaller.

That's the part we work with.
